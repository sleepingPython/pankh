Why Pankh?

Many of us grew up on stories from grandparents, summer holidays in India, neighbourhood games, and lessons tucked inside everyday adventures. Our children may be growing up far from those places, but stories can still open a window into that world.

Pankh means "wings". We want to give children the curiosity to explore, and the roots to come back to. Through our stories they meet familiar foods, places and family traditions, and see themselves in the characters, without it ever feeling like homework.

"The best way to pass on a culture isn't to tell children what to remember. It's to give them stories they'll want to remember."
